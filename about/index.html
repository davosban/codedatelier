Established in 2013. Coded Atelier and Events started as an event company which hosted occasional themed events in the United States of America. The company in 2015 got their own venue space so extended by adding an atelier, recreation, and event venue. In 2017, the company expanded to have other branches which now includes its sister company Coded Atelier, Recreation and Events located in Accra Ghana. 

Now aside our inhouse themed events, the company offers more programs. Also, the company has exquisite ever changing catered-in menu/cuisine, workroom, event planning, and space rental for all client requested events for any special celebrations with art incorporated.
