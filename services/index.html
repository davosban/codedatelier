Kids After School Care Program

Our kids after school care are offered from Mondays to Fridays on a weekly based calendar during the year. We aim to help your children build their communication skills, fine motor skills, gross motor skills, social skills, as well as boosting their leadership skills & time management. It will also show case your kids’ talent & help encourage fitness which goes towards their good health. We also watch your children after school closes and engage them in stimulating activities till they are picked up by you or dropped off by our transportation department if enrolled. Through all our programs & activities offered, your kids are guaranteed to boost their confidence, acquire great objectives & have a lot of fun.
